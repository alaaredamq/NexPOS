    What it does

    One shop day, start to finish.

    Everything a counter needs, in the order you need it. Nothing to learn twice: cashiers sign in with a 4-digit PIN and see only what their role allows.

    1. 07:45

      Open the drawer

      Count the float once. Coduo POS remembers what went in, so the evening count has something to match.

    2. 08:00

      Sell, fast

      Type a few letters or a code and the item is on the bill. Hold a basket while the customer fetches one more thing, add a discount, take cash or card, in more than one currency.

    3. 13:10

      Put it on the tab

      A regular pays at the end of the month? Their debt is written down with every sale and payment, and the balance is always one tap away.

    4. 16:30

      The delivery arrives Pro

      Record the purchase, and stock goes up on its own. Each sale takes it down again. Low-stock items warn you before the shelf is empty.

    5. 21:00

      Close the shift

      Count the drawer, see the difference, print the end-of-day report on the receipt printer. The day is locked and backed up.

    6. 21:30

      Check from home Online

      Today's sales, what's running low, which tills are connected. From your phone, wherever you are.

    Online

    Away from the shop, not out of touch.

    Your shop PC sends a copy of the day to Coduo POS Online every few minutes. Open it on your phone to see sales, stock and every branch you own.

    We can't read your numbers.

    The shop PC locks the copy with a key only you hold, printed on a recovery slip. Our server stores it. Your phone opens it. Cost prices, staff and cash counts never leave unlocked.

    Questions

    Asked at the counter.

    Do I need internet to sell?

    No. Coduo POS runs on the shop's own PC and keeps its data there. Selling, printing and closing the shift all work without internet. Internet is only used if you add Online.

    What computer and printer do I need?

    A Windows 10 or 11 PC. Any thermal receipt printer that speaks ESC/POS works, and a cash drawer that plugs into it opens on each cash sale. No printer? Receipts and reports save as PDF.

    Does it work in Arabic?

    Yes. Every screen, receipt and report is in English and Arabic, with a proper right-to-left layout. Each user picks their own language.

    Can I take payment in dollars and lira at the same time?

    Yes. Set your currencies and today's rate, and the bill shows the total in each. Change the rate whenever it moves.

    What if the PC breaks?

    Coduo POS backs itself up on a schedule. Restore the latest backup on a new PC, ask us for a key for that PC, and carry on where you stopped.

    What happens to my data if I move from Pro to Basic?

    Nothing is deleted. Pro pages lock until you upgrade again, and everything is still there when they unlock.

    Can my cashiers see costs and profits?

    Only if you let them. Each role sees its own pages, and every action is signed with the cashier's PIN.
