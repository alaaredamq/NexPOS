QUESTIONS AT THE COUNTER

Do I need internet to sell?

No. Coduo POS runs on the shop's own PC and keeps its data there. Selling, printing and closing the shift all work without internet. Internet is only used if you add Online.

What happens when the power cuts?

Every sale is written to the PC's disk the moment you press Pay, so a cut loses nothing that was paid. When the PC is back on, everything up to the last sale is there.

What computer and printer do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC. Any thermal receipt printer that speaks ESC/POS works, and a cash drawer that plugs into it opens on each cash sale. No printer yet? Receipts and reports save as PDF.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. Every screen, receipt and report is in English and Arabic, with a proper right-to-left layout. Each user picks their own language.

Dollars and lira on the same bill?

Yes. Set today's rate once and the bill shows the total in both. Take part in dollars and part in lira, and the change is worked out for you.

Can I bring my products from Excel?

Yes. Import the sheet you already keep, with names, prices, barcodes and stock, and fix anything after.

What if the PC breaks?

Restore the latest backup on the new PC, ask us for a key for it, and carry on where you stopped.

Can my cashiers see costs and profits?

Only if you let them. Each role sees its own pages, and every action is signed with the cashier's PIN.

Can you read my data when I use Online?

No. Your shop PC locks the data with a key that only you hold, printed on a recovery slip. Our server stores it but cannot open it.
